Our Story

Patisserie Plain bakery began with a dream shared by two friends who wanted to have their own cake shop. We started by planning our business, saving money, and learning how to bake and decorate mini cakes.

Although we faced challenges, we never gave up. Through teamwork, patience, and hard work, we turned our dream into a growing mini-cake business. We aim to make every celebration sweeter with delicious and affordable mini cakes.

Our cake shop is a sweet dream that we built together!
